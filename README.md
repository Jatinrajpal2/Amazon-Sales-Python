# 🛒 Amazon Sales Analysis – Python

An end-to-end Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) project using Python to analyze Amazon e-commerce transaction data and 
generate meaningful business insights related to sales, profitability, products, geography, seasonal trends, and shipping efficiency.

## 📌 Project Overview

This project delivers an end-to-end exploratory data analytics (EDA) study on Amazon e-commerce transaction data using Python. 
By examining detailed order logs spanning product hierarchies, sales channels, spatial distributions, and fulfillment timelines, 
this analysis transforms raw transactional records into actionable business intelligence. The project focuses on uncovering revenue drivers, 
evaluating profit dynamics, identifying seasonal trends,and assessing operational fulfillment efficiency to help optimize inventory, marketing strategy, and logistics.

## 🎯 Objective

The primary objectives of this project are:

1. To analyze sales performance across different product categories, regions (Country, State, City), and time periods (Month, Year) to identify top-performing segments and seasonal trends.
2. To evaluate profitability by examining Profit and Profit Margin% across categories, products, and geographic locations, identifying the most and least profitable segments of the business.
3. To assess operational efficiency by analyzing Ship Time and its correlation with customer regions, helping identify potential bottlenecks in the delivery process.
4. To identify key growth drivers by studying the relationship between Sales, Quantity, and Profit to understand which products/categories contribute most to revenue and profit generation.
5. To visualize data trends through interactive charts and dashboards, making complex data patterns easily interpretable for business stakeholders.
6. To provide data-driven recommendations that can support strategic decisions related to inventory planning, regional expansion, pricing strategy, and logistics optimization.
7. To build a reusable analytical framework using Python that can be scaled or adapted for ongoing sales performance monitoring.

## 📊 Key Insights

- 💰 **Copiers** generated the highest total profit among the analyzed categories, with **19,327.24**.
- 📅 **December** recorded the highest monthly sales at **115,934.54**, followed by **November** with **92,988.92**.
- 📍 **Washington** recorded **138,641.27** in sales and **33,402.65** in profit.
- 📦 **Staples** had the highest total quantity sold among the products analyzed.
- 🚚 **Ship Time** was analyzed to understand delivery efficiency and its relationship with business performance.

## 💡 Business Recommendations

🔹 Focus on high-performing categories such as **Copiers** to strengthen profitability.
🔹 Review loss-making products and improve their pricing, discounts, or cost structure.
🔹 Increase inventory and marketing efforts during high-sales months, especially November and December.
🔹 Focus on high-volume categories such as Binders to maintain strong sales performance.
🔹 Prioritize California and Washington markets due to their strong sales and profit contribution.
🔹 Optimize shipping operations, although the analysis shows very weak relationship between shipping time and profit.
